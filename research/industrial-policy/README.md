# Industrial Policy, Industrial Parks, and Poverty Reduction

Research topic opened 2026-05-03. Continued with full-text source fetches
on the same date.

## Question

How should we think about China's industrial parks as a poverty-reduction
instrument — what worked, what didn't, and what are the structural pros and
cons relative to Southeast Asia (SEA)?
