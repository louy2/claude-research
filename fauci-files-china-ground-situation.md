# The "Fauci Files": How Much Did He Know About the COVID-19 Situation Inside China in January 2020?

*Research note — compiled 2 August 2026. Revised to incorporate the July 2026 diary release.*

## The question

Based on the documents now grouped as the "Fauci files," how much did Dr. Anthony Fauci
actually know about the **epidemiological situation on the ground inside China** — case
numbers, hospital strain, human-to-human and asymptomatic transmission, severity, and
when the outbreak really began — during **January 2020**?

This is deliberately narrower than the better-known lab-origin debate. Origins material
appears here only where it bears on the timeline of what Fauci knew about the outbreak
itself.

anchor is the newest one:

## Short answer

**Fauci knew more, and earlier, than the reassuring public messaging of January 2020
suggested — and the newly released diary makes this much more concrete than the emails ever
did.** Specifically, on the primary record he privately treated **sustained human-to-human
transmission as settled by 20 January 2020**, and by **26 January** had concluded that the
first infection dated to **early December 2019**, that the Huanan market was "the amplifier"
rather than the source, and that spread had been running **for weeks before China disclosed
anything**.

But the record does not support the simplest "he knew and hid it" story, for three reasons:

1. **On China's conduct, the diary broadly matches what he said publicly.** He criticized
   Chinese transparency on the record in late January 2020 and repeatedly afterward. The
   private/public gap on *this* topic is narrow.
2. **Where he was wrong in public, the diary shows him wrong in private too** — most
   importantly on asymptomatic transmission, where he was privately *skeptical* of China's
   own claim, not sitting on it (§4).
3. **The severity gap runs the other direction from the usual accusation:** privately he
   estimated a *lower* fatality rate than he was publicly associated with, and he published
   that lower estimate in the NEJM three weeks before the testimony it is said to contradict
   (§5).

The single sharpest finding for this question is the **3 February 2020 call with George Gao**,
in which China's own CDC director confirmed asymptomatic transmission and Fauci recorded that
Wuhan officials had withheld the truth from Beijing, a lapse that "likely allowed the outbreak
to take hold" (§4).

**Important limit:** there is **no December 2019 content in the release at all.** Everything
Fauci wrote about December 2019 is retrospective, written in late January and February 2020.

## 1. What the diary records about the scale of the outbreak

Fauci kept a near-daily running tally of Chinese cases and deaths. This is the richest and
least-reported vein in the release. Verbatim, with internal page stamps:

| Entry | Recorded | p. |
|---|---|---|
| Jan. 16, 2020 | "The situation in Wuhan, China continues. Now have 2 deaths and 2 cases in Thailand and 1 in Japan." | 672 |
| Jan. 18, 2020 | "There are now 17 more cases in Wuhan and the LSTMH has estimated that there may actually be several hundreds of cases that have not yet been recognized." | 673 |
| Jan. 19, 2020 | "Now there are 198 confirmed cases, all in China except for 2 in Thailand and 1 in Japan." | 673 |
| Jan. 21, 2020 | "Now 298 cases with 6 deaths. Very fluid." | 673 |
| Jan. 25, 2020 | "now more than1400 cases and 42 deaths. President Xi Jinping essentially sutting down the entire country." | 676 |
| Jan. 30, 2020 | "9480 with 213 deaths. It is increasing by about 25% per day. In 1 month it has surpassed SARS for 1 year (8000)." | 679 |
| Feb. 3, 2020 | "Cases = 17,205 with 361 deaths" | 683 |
| Feb. 10, 2020 | "97 people die in China yesterday – most for a single day." | 688 |
| Feb. 13, 2020 | "60,349/1369" | 691 |
| Feb. 19, 2020 | "75,201/2012 … 1700 health care workers have been infected." | 695 |

On hospital strain and state response (**Jan. 26, 2020**, p. 676):

> "There is complete chaos in China now with president Xi Jinping declaring an emergency
> situation. The Chinese are building within days 1000 and 1300 bed hospitals and flying in
> hundreds of medical personnel from military. They have shut down travel within most of the
> country and have called off many Lunar New Year events. Schools closed at least until
> mid-February. Running out of supplies such as PPE."

The Jan. 18 entry matters for the "what did he know" question: he was already recording an
outside estimate (London School of Hygiene & Tropical Medicine) that **true case counts were
in the hundreds** while China's official figure was a few dozen. He was not relying on
Chinese numbers alone.

## 2. Human-to-human transmission

The progression in the diary is unusually crisp.

- **Jan. 3, 2020** — "No human-to-human transmission documented at this time."
- **Jan. 18, 2020** (p. 673) — first hint: "It is starting to look like there is some low
  grade close contact transmission person to person."
- **Jan. 20, 2020** (p. 673) — settled, and the driver was infected clinicians:

  > "Now there are >220 cases of n-CoV with a travel case in South Korea and **14
  > transmissions to health care providers. Clearly, this is a rapidly expanding situation
  > with no question about person-to-person transmission.**"

- **Jan. 26, 2020** (p. 676) — retrospective, on having been misled: "Remember, early on the
  Chinese were saying that there is no human to human transmission and all the original 27
  cases were from the market."
- **Feb. 3, 2020** (p. 683) — "26 countries have 180 international cases and within that there
  are 20 human to human transmission transmissions with no sustained transmission yet."

*Context not from the diary:* Chinese authorities publicly acknowledged human-to-human
transmission on 20 January 2020, via Zhong Nanshan. Fauci's private conclusion is therefore
**contemporaneous with**, not ahead of, China's own admission — which weakens any claim that
he was privately sitting on undisclosed transmission data that month.

## 3. The 26 January 2020 entry — outbreak timing and the market

The most-quoted entry in the release, and the one that matters most for this question
(p. 676, verified verbatim):

> "It now appears using epi data and genomic data that **the first infection was in early
> December and was not connected to the market. The infections spread among people weeks
> before the Chinese reported that they were dealing with a new infection and that gave the
> virus time to establish itself in multigenerational (sustained) spread.** Remember, early
> on the Chinese were saying that there is no human to human transmission and all the
> original 27 cases were from the market. **Now we know the market was not the source, it was
> the amplifier.** Having said that, **somewhere the virus jumped from animals to humans.**"

Two things follow, and they point in opposite political directions:

- **For the "he knew" case:** by 26 January he had privately concluded China lost the world
  weeks of warning.
- **Against the lab-leak reading of this entry:** the same passage ends by asserting **animal-to-human
  spillover**. Sen. Paul cites this entry as private lab-leak sympathy; read whole, it is a
  zoonosis statement with a corrected index-case timeline.

FactCheck.org adds that this was not secret knowledge: *The Lancet* had published two days
earlier that roughly a third of early confirmed cases had no market link, and Fauci said
essentially the same thing publicly to Hannity on 27 January 2020 and to USA Today on
17 February 2020 ("the market was an amplifier of something that was already going on").

## 4. Asymptomatic transmission — and the Gao call

This is where the "did he know?" framing most needs correcting. In January he was **privately
skeptical of China's own asymptomatic-transmission claim** — the opposite of suppressing it.

**Jan. 26, 2020** (p. 676):

> "Dr. Ma Xiaowei, the Chinese Minister of Health gave a press statement saying that the
> incubationperiod of nCoV is 1 to 14 days and that the virus is being transmitted when people
> are asymptomatic! This has enormous implications for how we handle people from Wuhan …
> **I am very skeptical about the Chinese interprepation of their data.** I spoke with Mike
> Ryan (WHO) from Geneva … Mike is very skeptical that aymptomatic spread is present or at
> worst is a major driver of spread. Mike Osterholm also feels this way."

Same entry — he wanted American epidemiologists on the ground:

> "Bob and I feel (and I told Elizabeth Cohen this) that **we should be allowed to send our
> CDC epidemiologists to China to work with them so that we see the data first hand**."

**Jan. 28, 2020** — "Chinese scientists are saying that the epidemic is much worse than is
being reported. Dr. Yuan say that 50% of the infections are asymptomatic … A lot of confusion
and misinformation while the outbreak accelerates."

### The 3 February 2020 call with George Gao

The single most important entry for this question (pp. 683–684, verified in the text layer and
in the rendered page image):

> "Spoke with George Gao tonight in China. George is the Director General of the Chinese
> Centers for Disease Control and Prevention. **He confirmed that infected people can be
> asymptomatic and that asymptomatic people can transmit.** … Also, George was very sensitive
> and understandably defensive since he was the one who said early on that there was no human
> to human transmission and that the original 27 cases in the wet market reported on December
> 31, 2019 were animal to human when actually human to human transmission was occurring weeks
> before. **The Wuhan health officials did not tell him this and he thus gave out false
> information. This lapse likely allowed the outbreak to take hold since Wuhan went about
> their business with 40,000 pot luck festivals without social distancing.**"

**Three corrections to how this entry is circulating:**

1. **"He … gave out false information" refers to Gao, not Fauci.** The grammar is unambiguous
   in context: Gao made the false claim because Wuhan officials withheld from him. Paul's
   compressed quotation invites misreading this as a Fauci self-incrimination. It is not.
2. **The document's own date stamp is a typo — it reads "Feb. 3, 2019."** It sits between an
   entry stamped "Feb. 2, 2020" and one stamped "Jan. 4, 2020" (itself a typo for Feb. 4, fixed
   by its case counts and a State of the Union reference). Feb. 3, 2020 is contextually certain,
   but cite the page stamp alongside the date.
3. **Fauci's stated mechanism is local:** the outbreak took hold "since Wuhan went about their
   business with 40,000 pot luck festivals" — he blames unmitigated mass gatherings and
   **Wuhan municipal officials deceiving Beijing's own CDC**, whom he treats sympathetically
   ("understandably defensive"). That is a narrower claim than "China deceived the world."

**Follow-through on his own error.** By **Feb. 4, 2020** (stamped "Jan. 4, 2020," p. 684) he
recorded that the NEJM paper he had relied on for asymptomatic transmission was flawed, and
that **"I quoted that paper (now flawed) in the WH presser."**

## 5. Severity — the gap runs the other way

**Feb. 8, 2020** (p. 687):

> "Tom Frieden called me this AM … He and I are on the same page in thinking tht this is acting
> like a bad influenza in its transmissibility and that the denominator is much greater than
> 34,867 (above) **making the case fatality rate (CFR) more like 0.2-0.3 % rather than 2.0%.**"

Fox News and others frame this as contradicting his 11 March 2020 testimony (~1%, "10 times
more lethal" than seasonal flu). **That framing omits a decisive fact:** Fauci, Lane and
Redfield published the same low estimate **publicly** in the *New England Journal of Medicine*
on **28 February 2020** — three weeks *before* the testimony:

> "If one assumes that the number of asymptomatic or minimally symptomatic cases is several
> times as high as the number of reported cases, the case fatality rate may be considerably
> less than 1%" — and consequences "may ultimately be more akin to those of a severe seasonal
> influenza."

So the private estimate was not hidden; he printed it. Treat "private vs. public CFR
contradiction" as **contested and materially incomplete as usually presented.**

## 6. The Fauci–Gao WeChat exhibit — what it actually says

Widely reported as proof that "Fauci was told by February 2020 that COVID was already adapted
to humans." I decoded the Select Subcommittee exhibit (`SSCP_NIAID000022`) directly. The full
message reads:

> "Dear George, as the world is dealing with the highly transmissible SARS-2 delta variant, I
> have never forgotten what you said to ASF on the phone call in early Feb 2020. You said
> although we don't know much about the new coronavirus one thing you do know is the new virus
> 'adapted human host well'. I remember it. Now your early observation has proven to be true…"

**The critical caveat, which most coverage omits: the message is dated 29 July 2021 — not
February 2020.** It is a **recollection written eighteen months later** by a third party
(Ping Chen, NIAID's representative at the U.S. Embassy in Beijing, who arranged the call) to
Gao, referring to Fauci in the third person as "ASF." It is not a contemporaneous record of
what Fauci was told, and Gao's reply is not in the exhibit. Note also that the exact phrase is
**"adapted human host well"**, not the smoother "adapted to human hosts well" that appears in
secondary reporting.

*Related, and worth flagging for fairness:* on **16 June 2021** Fauci's diary dismisses
Robert Redfield's argument that the virus "was so well adapted to humans right from the
beginning that it had to have leaked from a lab" as "total nonsense." That rejects Redfield's
**inference** (well-adapted ⇒ engineered), not the underlying observation — but anyone pairing
the WeChat exhibit with a claim of concealment should engage this entry.

## 7. China's candour and the WHO thread

- **Jan. 24, 2020** (p. 675) — he *knocked down* a story alleging Chinese pressure on WHO:
  "Call from Lena Sun (Washington Post) trying to verify … that the Chinese got to Tedros to
  convince him not to declare a PHEIC … **I told her that was nonsense and I probably stopped
  her from writing the story.**"
- **Jan. 25, 2020** (p. 676) — **transcription hazard.** The page literally reads: "**Suspicion
  that the Chinese are telling us everything that is going on in China.** Why are the Chinese
  reacting so dramatically by essentially shutting down the entire country?" As written this is
  incoherent; the following sentence shows he meant "are **not** telling us everything." The
  word "not" is genuinely absent from the document. Do not quote this without the caveat.
- **Jan. 31, 2020** (p. 682) — why WHO was rejected as convener: "Azar did not like the idea of
  WHO as convener because he feels (somewhat unfairly, I believe) that Tedros would immediately
  tell the Chinese and they would crush the attempt."
- **Feb. 12, 2020** (p. 691) — the sharpest passage, in Fauci's own voice:

  > "**Still no invitation for US representation from China to be part of group.** Azar getting
  > very angry and blaming Tedros for not pushing with China to get us on the group. Azar feels
  > that Tedros in bowing to Xi Jinping who does not want USA. **Tough situation since Tedros
  > and WHO depends greatly on China and often bows to them.**"

- **Feb. 13, 2020** (p. 691) — skepticism of Chinese case definitions: "Sharp increase in that
  they are not using just lab test for DX; they are using chest x-rays and CT scans of lung."
- **Feb. 19, 2020** (p. 695) — Mike Ryan's warning that China's suppression was "somewhat
  artificial" and that countries unable to be "so draconian" might see resurgence.

## 8. Document-integrity caveats (important, and largely unreported)

Anyone using this release for "he knew X on date Y" claims needs these:

1. **No December 2019 entries exist.** Despite the release being billed as covering "December
   2019 through December 2022," the earliest dated entry is **6 January 2020**. All December
   2019 content is retrospective.
2. **~200 stamped pages covering July 2015 – January 2020 are unreleased.** The Prequel ends
   near p. 465; the main package begins at stamped p. 671.
3. **A whole section is missing.** The text contains the marker "Part 6 – Continued from Part 5"
   after the 30 November 2021 entry, and the next entry is 31 May 2022 — **the entire Omicron
   surge (Part 5) is absent.**
4. **Date stamps are unreliable.** Verified typos include "Feb. 3, 2019" (= Feb. 3, 2020),
   "Jan. 4, 2020" (= Feb. 4, 2020), "Feb. 5, 2000," and "Jan. 28, 2019."
5. **Retrospective re-narration demonstrably occurs.** The contemporaneous 1 February 2020
   entry says most participants on the origins call thought "deliberate insertion was possible";
   a 7 June 2021 entry re-narrating the *same call* says "about half." Both are in the same
   document, and partisans quote whichever suits them.
6. **Two versions exist.** The PDF was posted 25–26 July, pulled the evening of 27 July, and
   reposted 28 July **with redactions**. NOTUS documented 19 removed references to one
   individual. Whether any China material differs between versions is unverified.
7. **Provenance is unexplained.** No one has said how Paul obtained the diary; HHS Secretary
   Kennedy said it was assembled "out of 11 separate servers," which bears on whether this is a
   single contemporaneous journal. Fauci has neither authenticated nor disputed it.

## 9. What Fauci has said in response

Essentially nothing substantive: at the 29 July 2026 HSGAC hearing he read a statement and then
**invoked the Fifth Amendment** (reported 100+ times), saying it "pains me to do so … because of
the respect I have for the legislative branch," and that Paul's "sole reason" was to make him
"say something that could vindicate his repeated public pledges that I end up … 'behind bars.'"
Paul has since moved toward a contempt vote and a DOJ referral.

**Correction to a widely repeated claim:** Fauci did **not** say the lab-leak scenario was
"molecularly impossible" at this hearing. That phrase is from his **3 June 2024** House
testimony, and concerns the EcoHealth/Wuhan grant, not his knowledge of the outbreak.

His representatives' rebuttal is the one directly relevant point: they argue the diary matches
his contemporaneous public statements — and on China specifically, **that holds up.** He publicly
criticized Chinese transparency on 27 January 2020 and again in April 2020 ("another example of
the unfortunate situation of lack of transparency early on").

## 10. Bottom line

On the ground situation inside China in January 2020, the newly released diary shows a Fauci who
was **better informed, earlier, and considerably more alarmed in private than the "very low risk"
public messaging of that month conveyed.** He had settled on person-to-person transmission by
20 January, and by 26 January had privately reconstructed a December 2019 origin, a market that
merely amplified, and weeks of spread China never disclosed. On 3 February, China's own CDC
director confirmed asymptomatic transmission to him and Fauci concluded that Wuhan's concealment
"likely allowed the outbreak to take hold."

What the record does **not** show is concealment of that assessment. On China's candour his
private and public positions substantially align; on asymptomatic spread he was privately wrong
in the same direction he was publicly wrong, and he documented correcting himself; on severity he
published his lower private estimate. The gap that the documents actually establish is between
**his private alarm about China in January and the calm risk framing offered to the American
public** — a gap he partly attributed at the time to genuinely bad Chinese data, and one he was
simultaneously urging the President to close.

Whether that gap is ordinary evolving-evidence lag or something worse remains contested, and the
release's integrity problems — no December 2019 entries, a missing section, unreliable date
stamps, two versions, unexplained provenance, and one entry proven to be retrospective — mean
this document cannot settle it on its own.
